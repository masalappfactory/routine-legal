4. Sharing, invitations, and your content

Sharing uploads the selected category and its relevant routines and history to Firebase. Joining requires confirmation of the invitation preview. Each category code can be used by multiple invitees for 24 hours unless revoked earlier. Protect codes: someone with a valid code can join with its assigned permissions.

Owners manage membership and permissions. Editors can make permitted changes; viewers are read-only. Authorized members can see shared content and retain or export copies. Revoking a code stops new joins with that code, not existing membership. Removing a member stops future server access but cannot recall copies already obtained. Deleting an owned category removes it from the service for everyone.

You retain rights to your content and allow Rutiniq and its providers to process, store, synchronize and display shared content as needed for the requested service, access control and legal obligations. Share only information you have the right and necessary permissions to share. Do not submit unlawful, abusive, infringing or unauthorized personal information.

5. Free use and Premium

Free use includes one category you own and up to three routines across categories you own. Archived or paused owned routines count toward the creation limit; occurrences and checklist items are not separate routines. Joining categories is free. Joined categories and role-permitted routines within them do not consume these personal ownership limits.

Premium includes unlimited owned categories and routines, creating sharing invitations, detailed insights and PDF reports. The paywall may appear when you request Premium features or exceed a Free creation limit. Existing content and history are not deleted solely because Premium ends; role-permitted access to existing shared content remains subject to category permissions. New Premium actions require an active entitlement. An invitation does not grant another member their own Premium subscription.
