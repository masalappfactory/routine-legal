Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how Routine Tracker - Rutiniq (Rutiniq, we, us) handles information in the iPhone app, its shared-category services, and these legal pages. For privacy requests, contact masalappfactory@gmail.com.

1. Personal information on your iPhone

Personal categories, routines, checklists, schedules, notes, history, achievements, preferences, and your display name are stored locally. Personal routine content is not automatically uploaded to our Firebase service. We cannot read records that remain only on your device.

Onboarding asks for a display name for Today and reminder messages. A nickname is sufficient; a legal name is not required. You can edit it in Settings. A random local identifier associates records with this installation. The widget uses a local App Group copy of relevant routine information.

If you allow notifications, iOS schedules reminders that may appear on your lock screen. You can hide routine names or disable notifications in Settings. Device backups may separately include app data according to your Apple settings.

2. Information shared with category members

When you share a category or join by invitation, Firebase processes that category and its routines, checklists, schedules, assignments, notes, and history for synchronization. Authorized members can see shared content, display names, roles, and completion authorship according to their permissions. A local copy supports offline use.

An invitation preview shows the category information needed to decide whether to join; joining requires your confirmation. Owners manage access, editors can make permitted changes, and viewers are read-only. Health or family information that you put in a shared note is visible to authorized members. Share only information you have permission to share. The app does not access HealthKit or obtain health records from other apps.

Each category has one active code, usable by multiple invitees for 24 hours unless revoked earlier. We process codes and operational records to validate invitations and prevent abuse. A permanent non-reversible code digest prevents reissuing an old code; this reservation contains no category content or user identifier.

3. Anonymous identity and reinstallation

There is no email/password registration. Firebase creates a random, pseudonymous identity for sharing. We do not use a device UUID or IDFV to recognize a returning installation. Each new installation starts as a new app user and does not automatically recover an earlier sharing identity.

Uninstalling does not request deletion of server-side shared data. Use Settings → Delete account and data before uninstalling if you want that data removed. A private backup can restore supported personal records, but cannot recover shared ownership or a previous sharing identity. Purchase restoration is separate from routine-data recovery.

4. Purchases and subscription information

Apple handles App Store payment details. RevenueCat verifies purchases and Premium access using its own anonymous customer identifier, purchase and transaction information, subscription status and lifecycle events, app and OS information, store, locale, and currency. Purchase information also supports subscription reporting and fraud prevention. RevenueCat is contacted at app startup even if you use only personal routines.

We do not send routine content, display names, invitation codes, or Firebase identifiers to RevenueCat as customer attributes. We do not receive your payment-card details. We do not collect IDFA or IDFV for advertising or enable cross-app advertising tracking. Apple or RevenueCat may recognize a purchase during restoration; this does not recover your former sharing account.

5. Service operation, permissions, and analytics

Network requests expose IP addresses and request metadata to the receiving provider. Firebase Authentication handles authentication information; Firestore and Cloud Functions handle shared-data requests; App Check handles integrity attestations and tokens. Operational information can include request times, status, random service identifiers, and abuse-prevention counters. It is used for requested functionality, access control, security, troubleshooting, and support.

No separate behavioral usage-analytics SDK or Google Analytics event collection is included in this version. In-app insights and achievements are calculated from routine records on your device. RevenueCat subscription reporting is separate from routine-usage analytics.

Notification permission is requested when you choose Remind me during onboarding or enable reminders later. Refusal does not prevent routine tracking. Routine features do not request camera, contacts, SMS, location-services, microphone, or HealthKit access.

6. Backups and exports you choose

Creating or restoring a private backup and exporting CSV/PDF reports are Premium features. They run only when requested. Privacy requests and account deletion do not require Premium. Private backups exclude shared categories and membership credentials and are not password protected. Import uses the system file picker; export and sharing use the system share sheet. Copies saved to iCloud Drive, another provider, or another person are controlled by you and the recipient. App deletion cannot remove those independent copies.

7. Providers and international processing

Our providers are Google Firebase for shared storage, authentication, functions and integrity checks; RevenueCat for purchase verification, entitlements and subscription reporting; Apple for distribution, billing, operating-system services and device backups you enable; and GitHub Pages for hosting these documents. We use these providers for service purposes, not for selling or renting your personal information or targeted advertising.

Shared storage and functions are configured in europe-west1. Authentication, billing, security, and support processing may also occur in other countries under provider terms and applicable data-protection arrangements. Provider policies are linked below. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or service security, or in a service transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations.

8. Retention and deletion

Personal records stay on your device until you delete them, remove app data, or uninstall; separate backups and exports follow your own deletion choices. Shared content stays in the active service until deleted through the app or an account-deletion request. Leaving a category or losing a device does not erase that category for other members.

Settings → Delete account and data removes local data and requests removal of the sharing identity, owned shared categories, membership information, and your own content in other categories. Owned shared categories are removed for every member; other members’ own content in categories you do not own is preserved. Deletion requires no purchase or export. Network failures can delay server work; the request resumes and the app confirms completion.

Small security records can remain to reject old access, prevent offline clients from recreating deleted records, and confirm deletion. These include deletion markers, non-reversible invitation-code reservations, and a non-reversible random receipt digest; they do not retain deleted routine text. Completed deletion jobs are marked for expiry after 24 hours; expiry does not guarantee immediate physical removal by the storage provider.

Provider backups and operational logs follow their documented retention rules. Firebase states that deleted authentication information is removed from live and backup systems within up to 180 days. Content exported or cached by other members may remain until they delete it or their app reconnects.

Deleting the app or its sharing account does not cancel a subscription and does not automatically delete Apple or RevenueCat transaction records. Those records are processed separately for restoration, entitlement checks, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of subscription-related personal data. We will help identify the relevant anonymous record and request deletion where permitted; providers may retain records required by law. Do not send payment-card details.

9. Your choices and rights

You can edit your name, control notifications, export supported records, revoke invitations, leave categories, or delete your account in the app. Contact masalappfactory@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion or portability of information held by our service, or to object to or restrict processing where applicable law provides those rights. You may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Processing supports the services you request, legal obligations, and proportionate security and abuse-prevention interests. Optional permissions remain under your control. Where processing relies on consent, withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. We may reasonably verify requests because there is no registered email/password identity. We will not ask for your Apple password or payment-card details and will respond within applicable legal deadlines. Records stored only on your phone must be managed on that phone or through your own backup.

10. Children and other people’s information

Rutiniq is a general-purpose planning tool, not a service designed for children to independently submit personal information. Parents or guardians should supervise use where local law requires it. Adults managing family routines should avoid unnecessary identifying or sensitive information about children or other people, especially in shared categories. Contact us about information provided by a child without appropriate authorization so we can investigate and delete it where required.

11. Legal pages and support email

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or external fonts added by Rutiniq. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security as described by GitHub. External links lead to services with their own privacy practices. If you email us, we receive your address, message, and information you include and use them to answer your request and keep necessary support records.

12. Changes and contact

We update the effective date when this policy changes. Material changes will be communicated through the app or an appropriate notice on these pages where required. For support or privacy requests, contact Rutiniq at masalappfactory@gmail.com.
